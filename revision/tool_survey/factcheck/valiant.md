### Sources and interpretation

I inspected the complete `develop` tree at commit [`8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb`](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/commit/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb), the project wiki/documentation, the official Quay container page/API, repository history and metadata, and Barbon *et al.* 2022 ([local PDF](/mnt/c/Users/zhliu/Desktop/KinneyLab/25_poolparty_private/revision/tool_survey/papers/Barbon2022_VaLiAnT_all.pdf), journal pp. 892–899). “Documented CLI” below means an interface exposed in the current README/CLI. “Documented workflow” means the documentation uses core CLI pieces for that purpose, but there is no separate command. “Example/manual only” means the repository demonstrates a surrounding step that VaLiAnT itself does not automate. Source-code-only observations are called out explicitly.

VaLiAnT generates **DNA oligonucleotide sequences and variant/sequence metadata**. Its codon mutators explore protein-level substitutions by choosing DNA codons, but it does not emit a protein-sequence library or perform downstream variant-effect scoring ([README overview, lines 1–5](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L1-L5); paper pp. 895–898).

### Library-generation and mutation capabilities

| Capability | What it does; interface and documentation status | Restrictions / boundaries | Primary evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Genomic/Saturation Genome Editing mode | `valiant sge OLIGO_INFO REF_FASTA OUTPUT SPECIES ASSEMBLY` retrieves local genomic reference segments from coordinates and generates targeton-specific oligo libraries. A BED-like TSV defines a full reference span, central `r2`, lengths of `r1`/`r3`, mutator sets for all three regions, strand, and sgRNA IDs. Multiple targetons and multiple mutators per region are accepted. **Documented CLI.** | Requires a local FASTA plus `.fai`; chromosome naming must match annotation/reference. `SPECIES` and `ASSEMBLY` are metadata strings, not remote reference selectors. Output directory must already exist. | [README, lines 65–120](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L65-L120), [191–240](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L191-L240), [630–657](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L630-L657); paper pp. 893–896. |
| cDNA DMS mode | `valiant cdna` takes user-provided entries in a multi-FASTA, relative targeton and mutable-`r2` coordinates, and optional CDS/gene/transcript annotation. Custom VCF variants, PAM edits, background variants, SGE VCF output, and minus-strand reverse-complement mode are not exposed here. If `r2` overlaps an annotated CDS, it must be wholly inside it. | [README, lines 191–196 and 245–253](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L191-L253), [659–699](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L659-L699), [741–743](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L741-L743); [`cdna_cli.py`, lines 26–75](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/cdna_cli.py#L26-L75); [`cdna_proc.py`, lines 78–107](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/cdna_proc.py#L78-L107); [cDNA wiki](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/wiki/cDNA-DMS-file-formats); paper pp. 895, 897. |
| Saturation prime-editing library construction | Uses ordinary `valiant sge`: treat the user-chosen reverse-transcription template (RTT) genomic window as a targeton, mutate it, optionally reverse-complement it, and put spacer/scaffold/PBS/cloning/primer sequences in the two adaptors to form full pegRNA oligos. All SGE mutators, custom variants, and user-supplied PAM/protospacer edits can participate. **Documented workflow with repository examples; no `prime` subcommand.** | VaLiAnT does not choose a guide, RTT/PBS window, spacer, scaffold, or protection edit; these must be designed upstream. | Region-specific action vectors | Each SGE targeton can comprise `c1-r1-r2-r3-c2`; the three mutable regions can receive independent comma-separated mutator sets, while outer sequence is constant. Duplicate mutator labels and spacing are ignored. Custom variants, unlike pattern mutators, may occur in the constant portions. **Documented CLI/input format.** | A generated pattern mutation is one event in one region/oligo; VaLiAnT does not form a combinatorial cross-product among generated pattern mutations or custom VCF variants. Fixed background and PAM changes can co-occur with that one variable event. | [README, lines 630–657](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L630-L657); paper pp. 893–896; independent custom/pattern insertion paths in [`targeton.py`, lines 204–312](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/targeton.py#L204-L312). |
| Exhaustive SNVs (`snv`) | At every eligible nucleotide, emits the three non-reference substitutions; for CDS, reports the resulting amino-acid change. **Documented mutator, SGE and cDNA.** | One SNV per oligo, not combinations. It can operate in coding, UTR, intronic, or unannotated sequence. | [README, lines 257–309](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L257-L309); paper pp. 894, 896. |
| Parametric deletion scanning (`<SPAN>del[<OFFSET>]`) | Deletes non-overlapping windows of any valid positive span, beginning at an optional offset; examples include `1del`, `2del0`, `2del1`. **Documented mutator, SGE and cDNA.** | Windows proceed upstream-to-downstream; no partial final window is emitted. Version 4 is broader than the paper’s named mono-/dinucleotide choices. Metadata retains legacy label `1del` for `1del0`. | [README, lines 272–292](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L272-L292); [CHANGELOG, line 23](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/CHANGELOG.md#L20-L24); paper pp. 894–896. |
| In-frame codon deletions (`inframe`) | Deletes each complete triplet in-frame across an eligible coding region. **Documented mutator, SGE and cDNA.** | CDS only; only complete codons wholly contained in the mutable region are acted on. | [README, lines 262–268 and 311–322](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L262-L322); paper pp. 894, 896. |
| Alanine scan (`ala`) | Replaces each complete codon with the highest-frequency alanine codon from the default or supplied codon table. **Documented mutator, SGE and cDNA.** | CDS only; an already-identical top alanine codon is a no-op and is skipped (source-observed). It produces DNA oligos, not protein records. | [README, lines 324–336](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L324-L336); [`mutators/codon.py`, lines 45–50 and 75–80](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/mutators/codon.py#L45-L80); paper p. 894. |
| Stop scan (`stop`) | Replaces each complete codon with the highest-frequency stop codon. **Documented mutator, SGE and cDNA.** | CDS only; an already-identical selected stop codon is skipped. | [README, lines 338–350](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L338-L350); [`mutators/codon.py`, lines 45–50 and 83–88](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/mutators/codon.py#L45-L88); paper p. 894. |
| Amino-acid saturation (`aa`) | For each complete codon, substitutes the highest-frequency codon for every other sense amino acid. With the default table this is 19 DNA variants per sense codon and 20 per reference stop codon. **Documented mutator, SGE and cDNA.** | CDS only; the reference amino acid and stop as an alternative are excluded. This is one preferred codon per amino acid rather than all synonymous DNA codons. | [README, lines 352–381](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L352-L381); [`mutators/codon.py`, lines 91–103](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/mutators/codon.py#L91-L103); paper p. 894. |
| SNV representation expansion (`snvre`) | Includes ordinary SNVs and adds codon replacements: synonymous SNVs are expanded to other synonymous codons; missense/nonsense outcomes get a preferred synonymous codon, using the second-ranked codon when the SNV itself is already top-ranked. This increases representation and tests codon sequence. **Documented mutator, SGE and cDNA.** | CDS only. Reference, the triggering SNV codon, and duplicates are excluded; amino acids with no additional synonymous codon yield no extra SNVRE variant. Ordinary `snv` is automatically included as a dependency. | [README, lines 383–463](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L383-L463); paper pp. 894–895. |
| Custom VCF-to-oligo conversion | Imports observed/user variants and emits an oligo for each variant across the whole targeton, including constant regions. Supports simple substitutions, insertions, deletions, and delins/indels. A CSV manifest accepts multiple VCFs, assigns source aliases, and gets identifiers from `ID` or a chosen `INFO` tag. **Documented SGE interface (`--vcf`).** | SGE only; records must fall wholly inside a targeton and are independent, not combinatorial. It is not a general structural-variant engine. Input alleles must be unambiguous DNA. Source code consumes only the first ALT of a multiallelic record, skips monomorphic records, and does not implement an insertion/deletion anchored at position 1. | [README, lines 465–476](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L465-L476), [701–718](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L701-L718); [`custom_variant.py`, lines 35–48 and 78–129](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/custom_variant.py#L35-L129); paper pp. 893, 895–896, 898. |
| Background-reference variants | `--bg` applies a VCF’s variants to create an alternate/background sequence before PAM edits and library mutations, and lifts coordinates across indels. Every generated oligo is therefore designed in that background. **Documented SGE CLI, added in 4.0.0.** | By default non-synonymous backgrounds and frame-shifting backgrounds are errors. `--force-bg-ns` permits the former; `--force-bg-indels` permits frameshifts and requires the non-synonymous override. Noncoding indels warn. Pattern/custom mutations overlapping coordinate-shifting background variants are discarded. A PAM/background overlap in the same coding codon is rejected. | [README, lines 86–120](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L86-L120); [CHANGELOG, lines 3–25](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/CHANGELOG.md#L3-L25); [`sge_proc.py`, lines 85–168](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/sge_proc.py#L85-L168), [306–320](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/sge_proc.py#L306-L320). |
| Background masking | `--bg-mask BED` excludes background records whose positions fall in specified intervals. **Documented SGE CLI.** | Masking a frameshift can itself alter consistency of downstream annotation; filtering is positional. The README’s CLI table accidentally calls the internal parameter `mask_bg_fp`, while the actual option is `--bg-mask`. | [README, lines 114–118](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L114-L118); [`sge_cli.py`, lines 26–54](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/sge_cli.py#L26-L54); [`sge_proc.py`, lines 131–168](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/sge_proc.py#L131-L168). |
| PAM/protospacer protection edits | `--pam` reads user-defined SNV protection edits linked to targetons by `SGRNA` identifiers and incorporates the applicable fixed edit(s) into every variable oligo. ### Annotation, sequence interpretation, and inputs

| Capability | What it does; interface and documentation status | Restrictions / boundaries | Primary evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coding consequence annotation | With `--gff` (SGE) or `--annot` (cDNA), VaLiAnT assigns gene/transcript context, reference/alternate codons and amino acids, and `syn`/`mis`/`non` mutation types to generated, custom, fixed PAM, and (where relevant) background changes. This also makes the tool useful as a VCF-to-oligo/consequence annotator. **Documented CLI/output.** | It is not a general genome consequence predictor: annotation is for the selected targeton/transcript and supported simple variants. | [README, lines 98–108](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L98-L108), [737–780](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L737-L780); paper pp. 893–898. |
| CDS boundary/codon-context handling | Current v4 can annotate SNVs in partial codons at a mutable-region edge by retrieving adjacent and even distal exonic bases; it supports out-of-frame CDS targeton regions with 5′/3′ extensions. Codon mutators act only on complete internal codons. **Core/documented by changelog and paper behavior.** | An individual mutable `r1`, `r2`, or `r3` may not mix CDS and non-CDS sequence or overlap multiple CDS exons. The complete targeton may span such boundaries by separating them into regions. | [CHANGELOG, lines 7–8](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/CHANGELOG.md#L5-L9); [`targeton.py`, lines 92–134](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/targeton.py#L92-L134); paper pp. 895–896. |
| GTF/GFF2 interpretation | SGE accepts GTF/GFF2, uses `CDS` and `UTR`, derives strand/frame/transcript context, and extends the last CDS by three bases to include the stop codon. **Documented SGE CLI/core.** | Only `CDS` and `UTR` feature rows are used. `gene_id` and `transcript_id` are required for validation, and the current loader rejects more than one gene/transcript pair for a contig/strand execution. ### Scope boundaries that affect any capability claim

- VaLiAnT is a **command-line generator/annotator**, with `sge` and `cdna` as the only documented subcommands. The repository has Python modules internally, but no stable public Python API is documented ([README CLI, lines 189–253](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L189-L253); [`pyproject.toml`, lines 23–24](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/pyproject.toml#L23-L24)).
- It designs each oligo around one variable pattern/custom event. It does **not** enumerate arbitrary combinations of independently variable mutations, optimize library diversity, choose degenerate codons, balance a synthesis pool, or score/filter designs by predicted function. Background and PAM edits are fixed context, not combinatorial variable dimensions (paper pp. 895–898; [`targeton.py`, lines 204–312](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/targeton.py#L204-L312)).
- Guide selection, targeton/RTT/PBS boundary selection, primer selection, PAM/protospacer edit design, tile design, and downstream DMS readout/scoring are upstream or downstream tasks. The paper explicitly describes them as possible integrations rather than VaLiAnT functions (paper p. 898; [prime-editing wiki](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/wiki/Saturation-prime-editing)).
- The current software differs materially from the 2022 paper: v2/v3/v4 subsequently added cDNA CLI restructuring, configuration replay, minimum-length filtering, additional output metadata/VCFs, arbitrary deletion span/offset, background variants, no-op controls, and improved out-of-frame CDS context. The frozen inventory therefore treats current 4.0.0 behavior as current and qualifies paper-era behavior ([CHANGELOG](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/CHANGELOG.md)).

## A. FACTUALLY WRONG

1. **The alleged hard-coded ordering of pattern and custom variants is wrong.** The record says: “**background variants → PAM/protospacer protection edits → mutator functions on r1/r2/r3 → custom VCF variants**” (lines 94–97). Background and PAM changes do form the shared base sequence, but `Targeton.process()` registers **custom variants first and pattern variants second**, and the two are independent one-event oligo branches rather than sequential transformations of one another. [`targeton.py`, lines 204–312](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/targeton.py#L204-L312)

2. **Built-in mutators do generate multi-base variants.** The record says: “**multi-base and combinatorial variants can only enter via a hand-authored VCF**” (lines 189–193). The combinatorial part is correct, but the multi-base part is false: arbitrary-span deletion mutators, `inframe`, `ala`, `stop`, `aa`, and `snvre` can all generate multi-nucleotide deletion or replacement events without a VCF. [README deletion and codon mutators, lines 272–463](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L272-L463)

3. **`--include-no-op-oligo` is not a general WT-inclusion switch.** The record calls it “**WT ('no-op') inclusion**” and says it adds a single sequence (lines 192–193). It emits a no-variable-change oligo only when at least one background or annotated PAM edit applies, and that oligo carries the fixed altered background/PAM sequence—so it need not be wild type. With no applicable fixed edit, the flag adds nothing. [`sge_cli.py`, lines 41–44](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/sge_cli.py#L41-L44); [`meta_table.py`, lines 414–457](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/meta_table.py#L414-L457)

4. **Not every execution writes `config.json`.** The record twice says “**each execution**”/“**per execution**” writes the configuration (lines 242–243 and 621–622). A normal library-generation run does, but `--sequences-only` writes `ref_sequences.csv` and calls `sys.exit(0)` before the common `finalise()` function writes the configuration. [`sge_proc.py`, lines 527–568](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/sge_proc.py#L527-L568); [`common_cli.py`, lines 94–115](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/common_cli.py#L94-L115)

5. **Current v4 does not consume GTF/GFF stop-feature rows.** The record says “**only CDS, UTR and stop features are taken into consideration**” (lines 320–322). The current loader recognizes only literal `CDS` and `UTR` feature types and ignores all others; it obtains stop context by extending the terminal CDS by three bases. This is a paper-era/current-software distinction that the record does not flag. [README, lines 104–108](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L104-L108); [`loaders/gtf.py`, lines 51–54 and 107–160](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/loaders/gtf.py#L51-L160)

6. **The record overstates enforcement of “one transcript per execution.”** It says “**the code confirms the wiki**” and concludes “**one transcript per run**” (lines 319–330, repeated at 632–635). That is the documented recommendation, but the current loader validates one gene/transcript pair only within the selected contig/strand group. `run_sge` loops over contigs, clears per-contig state, and merely warns—not errors—when annotation accompanies multiple contigs. Thus separate transcripts on separate contigs can proceed in one invocation; the implementation restriction is not globally “one per execution.” [`loaders/gtf.py`, lines 131–155](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/loaders/gtf.py#L131-L155); [`sge_proc.py`, lines 409–451 and 548–563](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/sge_proc.py#L409-L451)

7. **The SNVRE codon-ranking rule is misstated.** The record says `snvre` “**swaps a missense codon for the next most frequent triplet**” (lines 421–423). Current behavior is: use the top-ranked synonymous codon for the SNV’s amino-acid result, and use the second-ranked one only if the triggering SNV codon is already the top-ranked codon; nonsense changes follow the analogous stop-codon rule. [README, lines 383–463](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L383-L463); [`mutators/snv_re.py`](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/mutators/snv_re.py)

8. **Background-overlap filtering is narrower than the record claims.** It says “**Mutations that overlap background variants are discarded**” (lines 586–590 and 652). In the current implementation, pattern/custom variants are discarded by this overlap test for **coordinate-shifting background variants** (indels); the masks used by `ref_var_overlaps_var`/`alt_var_overlaps_var` are populated only where `alt_ref_delta != 0`. A mutation coincident with a background SNV is not discarded by that mechanism. [`genomic_position_offsets.py`, lines 108–146 and 246–277](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/genomic_position_offsets.py#L108-L146); [`targeton.py`, lines 204–227 and 278–290](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/targeton.py#L204-L290)

9. **SNVs are not the only generic variants that can affect partial/liminal codon bases.** The limitation says “**only SNVs reach them**” (line 640). Codon-specific replacement/deletion mutators do ignore partial codons, but the generic parametric deletion mutator and an imported custom variant can also affect those bases. [README mutator scope, lines 257–274](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L257-L274); [`mutators/deletion.py`, lines 32–48](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/mutators/deletion.py#L32-L48); [README custom variants, lines 465–476](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L465-L476)

10. **PAM protection edits are not restricted to synonymous or noncoding substitutions.** The record describes them as “**user-defined synonymous or non-coding SNVs**” (lines 573–577). That is the intended design pattern in the paper, not an input restriction in v4: the current parser requires a one-base REF/ALT and `SGRNA`, but accepts missense and nonsense edits too, which is why output supports `pam_mut_annot = syn|mis|non|ncd`. [`pam_variant.py`, lines 32–57](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/pam_variant.py#L32-L57); [README metadata, lines 774–775](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L774-L775)

11. **The two custom-provenance INFO tags are not carried by every output VCF record.** The record says the two output VCFs are “**each carrying `SGE_OLIGO`, `SGE_SRC`, `SGE_VCF_ALIAS`, `SGE_VCF_VAR_ID`**” (lines 591–593; similarly 368–370). `SGE_OLIGO` and `SGE_SRC` are general; `SGE_VCF_ALIAS` and `SGE_VCF_VAR_ID` are optional and apply only to imported custom variants, not ordinary mutator-generated records. [README, lines 789–803](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/README.md#L789-L803); [`vcf_writer.py`](https://github.com/cancerit/VaLiAnT/blob/8796cc112dafd4919fec59913f58cd2be87c45eb/src/valiant/vcf_writer.py)

## C. BALANCE

The record is **mostly fair, with a capability rather than limitation emphasis**. It gives generous, proportionate space to VaLiAnT’s real strengths—mixed region-specific mutagenesis, two design modes, background-aware design, prime-editing use, parametric deletions, rich provenance, two VCFs, deduplication, and examples—and devotes substantially more total space to capabilities/examples than to its compact limitations section. It is therefore not unfairly limitation-heavy. The main imbalance is inside the capability coverage: several absence claims and implementation details receive exhaustive treatment while the ordinary `snv`, `inframe`, `ala`, `stop`, and full `snvre` design semantics are largely name-only; the factual errors and omitted edge restrictions above also make the positive account somewhat more expansive than exact.
