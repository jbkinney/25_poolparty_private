\caption{\textbf{Scope of PoolParty and related tools.} Tools are grouped by the
problem they address. PoolParty specifies libraries of any type as a directed
acyclic graph (DAG) of operations, but it operates on sequences rather than genomic
annotations; VaLiAnT provides the genomic integration that PoolParty lacks.
\emph{Saturation mutagenesis} denotes enumeration of every single substitution at
every position of a region; \emph{randomly sampled variants} denotes variants drawn
by a rate or count parameter rather than enumerated. A capability-level matrix with
supporting evidence for every entry is given in Supplementary Table~S2.}
\label{tab:scope}

\toprule
\textbf{Tool} & \textbf{Designed for} & \textbf{How a library is specified} &
\textbf{Variants it can generate} & \textbf{Genomic context} \\
\midrule

\textbf{PoolParty} &
DNA sequence libraries of any type &
A directed acyclic graph of operations; the library is an object that reports its
exact size before any sequence is generated &
Saturation mutagenesis; pairwise and higher-order variants; randomly sampled
variants; combinatorial placement of motifs; several of these mixed in one library &
Sequences only; extracts regions from FASTA \\
\addlinespace

VaLiAnT &
Saturation genome editing and cDNA deep mutational scanning &
Tabular input, one row per target region; results written per region as CSV and VCF &
Saturation mutagenesis (substitutions, codon replacements, deletions of
user-specified length); several mutation types per target region &
Genome coordinates, transcript models, exon/intron structure, VCF output, variant
consequence annotation \\
\addlinespace

MPRAnator &
MPRA libraries of motif arrangements and sequence variants &
Web forms, one module per design type &
Randomly sampled and combinatorial substitution sets; combinatorial placement of
motifs &
Coordinates accepted; no transcript model \\
\addlinespace

MPRA Design Tools &
MPRA statistical power analysis and barcode assignment &
R function or Shiny app over a VCF &
One construct per input variant &
Coordinates via VCF; no transcript model \\
\addlinespace

Oligopool Calculator &
Library infrastructure (barcodes, primers, spacers) and sequencing readout &
Chained operations on tables &
Not in the API---variants are supplied by the user. \texttt{expand} enumerates
concrete sequences from a user-written degenerate template; the repository ships
example code for single-substitution scans &
None \\
\addlinespace

tangermeme &
Probing cis-regulatory logic with deep learning models &
Composable array functions; no library object &
Saturation mutagenesis; insertion and removal of motifs across sets of sequences &
Coordinates for sequence extraction \\
\addlinespace

General-purpose toolkits\newline\textit{(Biopython, pydna, SeqPro)} &
Sequence manipulation, cloning simulation, array preprocessing &
No library abstraction &
None built in &
Varies; Biopython supports coordinates and assembly liftover \\
\addlinespace

Adjacent design tools\newline\textit{(CodonGenie, DNA Chisel, ledidi, Mutation Maker)} &
Codon choice, synthesis-constraint optimisation, model-guided editing, mutagenic
primers &
Per sequence, not per library &
Edits to a single sequence; the user supplies the list of mutations &
None \\
