\noindent Dear Editor,

Thank you for considering our manuscript, ``\textit{PoolParty: streamlined design of DNA sequence libraries in Python},'' for publication as a Software Article in BMC Bioinformatics. As requested, the ``Data availability" section of the SNAPP submission system has been updated to include the text of the ``Availability of Data and Materials'' section of the manuscript, namely:

\begin{quote}
No experimental data were generated or analyzed in this study. The sequence and software used in the paper were obtained from public sources referenced in the manuscript: the GB1 coding sequence from Olson et al. (2014); the MPRA backbone, TFBS sequences, and inert background sequence from Melnikov et al. (2012) and Georgakopoulos-Soares et al. (2023); the SMN2 exon 7 genomic context from GRCh38.p13 (RefSeq assembly accession GCF\_000001405.39); the human 5' splice-site position weight matrix from Wong et al. (2018); MaxEntScan from Yeo and Burge (2004); and the pretrained SpliceAI model ensemble from Jaganathan et al. (2019).
\end{quote}
